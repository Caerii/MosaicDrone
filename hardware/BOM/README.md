# BOMs and Sourcing

## Contents
- Master BOMs per subsystem with MPNs and alternates
- Lifecycle, costs, lead times, QA specs

## Format
- CSV or YAML per subsystem
- Fields: Item, Qty, Description, MPN, Alt MPN, Supplier, Cost, LeadTime, Lifecycle, RoHS, Notes

## Process
- Change control via PR; approvals by hardware lead
- Supplier risk notes; second source policy
- QA incoming inspection criteria per line
