## Conventions
- Units: millimeters; ISO fits; surface finish marks
- Tolerances: general ±0.2 mm unless noted; hole tolerances per fit class
- Materials: CFRP, PETG-CF, 6061-T6, 304 SS; callouts on drawings
- Drawing format: A3 with title block: part no, rev, material, finish, tolerance
- Revisioning: semantic versioning; ECO log in `hardware/CHANGELOG.md`
- Assembly: exploded views and BOM balloons; torque specs on callouts
