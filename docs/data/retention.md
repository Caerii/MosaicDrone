# Data Retention Policy

## Retention periods
- Telemetry: 90 days (compressed); aggregates 1 year
- Maps: 1 year; landfill hazard maps 3 years
- Process logs (weld/print): 3 years for traceability
- Video: default disabled; if enabled, 30 days with redaction

## Storage
- Encrypted at rest; replicated; integrity via checksums

## Deletion
- Scheduled deletion; audit logs; legal holds pause deletion
