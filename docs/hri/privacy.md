# Privacy and Audit

## Data policy
- Collected: telemetry, maps, tool parameters, video (optional)
- Not collected: biometric identifiers; personal data beyond safety video
- Purpose limitation and minimization

## Access control
- Roles: operator, maintainer, auditor; least privilege
- Authentication: per-user keys; audit logs with tamper-evident storage

## Compliance
- Jurisdictional requirements; data subject rights (if applicable)
