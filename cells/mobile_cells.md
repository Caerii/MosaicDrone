# Mobile Recyclofacturing Cells

## Footprint
- Trailer/skid 3–6 m; 3–10 t; deploy time < 1 h
- Power: 24/48 V DC rails + 3-phase 208–480 VAC via generator or grid
- Filtration: welding fume extraction; HEPA for dust; VOC capture for plastics

## Processes
- Shred/sort: 1–5 t/h; magnetic + eddy + optical
- Cut: plasma up to 10 mm steel; waterjet optional; shear for sheet
- Weld: MIG/TIG; spot (if applicable); brazing; safety curtains
- Additive: WAAM 300–1000 mm envelope; FFF large-format 1–3 m

## Robotics
- 6–7 axis arms (10–25 kg payload) or gantry; reach 1–2 m; repeatability ≤ 0.1 mm
- EOATs: torches, cutters, grippers; quick-change

## Sensors
- Metrology: 3D scanning; thermal; cameras; NDT optional
- Environmental: gas (CH4/CO), particulates PM2.5/PM10, temperature

## Safety
- Interlocks; LOTO; fire suppression; zoning and light curtains; emergency stops
