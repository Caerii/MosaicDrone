# Toolheads Spec

## Family
- EM micro-gripper; clamp; screwdriver; riveter; marker; borescope

## Interfaces
- Mechanical: 2-point latch + anti-rotation key; ≤ 5 s changeover
- Power: 24 V DC up to 150 W peak (tool-dependent)
- Data: CAN or UART; status and sensor feedback

## Performance ranges
- EM gripper: 10–50 N hold on 1 mm steel, duty ≤ 50%
- Clamp: 100–300 N clamping force
- Screwdriver: 0.1–2.0 N·m torque; rpm 50–300; torque feedback
- Riveter: capacity per rivet spec; cycle time
- Marker: line width 2–10 mm; refillable
- Borescope: diameter ≤ 8 mm; LED illumination; video to SBC

## Safety
- Interlocks; pinch protection; tool-present sensing; E-stop integration

## Tests
- Life cycles; contamination; drops; EM interference
